🔑Signing In ▼

You can sign in two ways:

  • PRF number + PIN — use your WMAS PRF number and the 4-digit PIN your coordinator gave you.
  • Access Key — a code in the format cfr-word-word-1234. Use this if you don't have a PRF number, or if you're setting up a shared device.

Your PIN and access key are set by your coordinator. If you've forgotten them, ask your coordinator to reset your PIN.

Installing the app: Open the app in your phone's browser, then use "Add to Home Screen" (Safari on iPhone, or the browser menu on Android). The app will then work like a native app and function offline.

🕐Duty Hours ▼

Use this to log each duty session — the time you were available and on call.

  • Enter your start and end time, and the date.
  • Record how many incidents you were allocated (dispatched to) and how many you attended (arrived at scene).
  • Add any notes if relevant.
  • Submit when your duty is finished.

If you lose signal mid-duty, submit when you're back online — the form works offline and will sync automatically.

🚗Vehicle Shift (Crew Sign-On) ▼

This manages the live crew on the vehicle for each operational shift.

  • Starting a shift: The first person to sign on starts the shift. Enter your name, role (driver, observer, or lead responder), and tap Sign On.
  • Joining a shift: If a shift is already active, tap to join it with your role.
  • Ending the shift: The last person to sign off ends the shift automatically.

The active crew is shown on the compliance wallboard in real time.

✅Vehicle Daily Inspection (VDI) ▼

Complete a VDI each time you take the vehicle out. It covers 49 checks across 4 sections.

  • Tap All OK on a section to mark all items in it as passed.
  • Tap any individual item to toggle it if something needs flagging.
  • Record fuel level (0–8 bars) and oil level (0–4 bars) using the sliders.
  • Any item marked as failed will automatically create a defect entry.

Submit when you've completed the check. You don't need to be online — it'll sync when you reconnect.

📋Monthly Load List Check ▼

A monthly check of all equipment on the vehicle, based on WMAS Appendix 2 (~75 items).

  • Tap an item to mark it as present and checked.
  • For items with expiry dates (e.g. medications, consumables), expand the item and enter the month and year it expires.
  • Items expiring within 3 months show in amber; within 1 month in red.
  • Work through all sections and submit when complete.

On mobile: Tap the item name to toggle it — use the expiry dropdowns separately to enter dates without accidentally toggling the item.

📄Mileage & Incident Claims ▼

Submit mileage claims for WMAS reimbursement after attending incidents.

  • Enter the incident date, WMAS incident number (if known), start and end mileage, and any notes.
  • Submit after each incident, or batch multiple at once.

Your coordinator can export all claims as a CSV for submission to WMAS.

⚠️Defect Register ▼

Report any vehicle or equipment defects you find.

  • Choose whether it's a vehicle or equipment defect.
  • Describe the defect clearly and submit.
  • If you spot the same defect that's already been reported, re-report it — this bumps the timestamp so coordinators know it's still outstanding.

Coordinators and compliance officers can mark defects as resolved with a note. Resolved defects are hidden by default but can be shown with the toggle.

🔔Fire Safety Checks ▼

Three checks to keep the base compliant with fire safety regulations:

  • Fire Alarm Test — weekly. Activate each call point in turn and confirm the panel responds. Record any faults.
  • Emergency Lighting Test — monthly. Simulate a mains failure (press the test button on each fitting) and confirm all emergency lights activate. Record any that fail.
  • Fire Extinguisher Check — annual. Inspect all extinguishers for weight, pressure, damage, and valid service date. Only Fire Safety Officers and coordinators can submit this check.

Results are visible to coordinators in the Admin → Fire Safety tab.

📚Training Log ▼

Record your training sessions to maintain a personal log.

  • Enter the date, type of training (e.g. CPR refresher, scenario training, online module), duration, and any notes.
  • Your entries are visible to your coordinator.
📅Availability / Rota ▼

Mark periods when you are unavailable to respond.

  • Select the date range and reason (holiday, work, other).
  • Your unavailability is visible to your coordinator when planning the rota.
  • Vehicle unavailability (e.g. service, maintenance) is shown as a red banner across the affected dates.
📺Compliance Wallboard ▼

A display page designed for a shared screen or tablet in the base, showing at a glance:

  • Vehicle compliance status: MOT, road tax, service, insurance, deep clean — shown as green / amber / red cards.
  • Who is currently on duty.
  • Incident statistics: today, this month, this year, and duty hours this month.

Access via /wallboard.html using the 4-digit PIN set by your coordinator. The page refreshes automatically every 5 minutes.

The wallboard doesn't require a user login — the PIN is all that's needed.

📶Offline Use ▼

The app is designed to work without an internet connection.

  • All forms can be submitted offline — they're queued locally and sync automatically when you reconnect.
  • The badge at the top of the screen shows how many submissions are waiting to sync.
  • You'll see a toast notification when the app comes back online and syncs your data.

Install the app (Add to Home Screen) to make it available even with no signal. This is recommended for all responders.
